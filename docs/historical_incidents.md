# Historical Pit Incidents & Incident Memory Case Archive

[INC-CASE-01] 2024-03-12 Bench 4B Detachment (True Event):
Precursor: 72 hours of persistent rainfall followed by a 2.8 mm/hr displacement velocity acceleration over 4 hours. TARP Level 3 triggered at 04:15. Pit cleared of haul trucks. A 350 m3 planar block detached at 07:42. Zero injuries. Post-incident audit confirmed inverse velocity accurately flagged critical failure window.

[INC-CASE-02] 2024-07-19 Zone 2 Production Blast Spike (False Alarm):
Production blast at South Wall produced dense dust clouds and 42 mm/s PPV. Optical camera change detector issued automated Red Warning due to dust occlusion. Physical inspection confirmed zero rock displacement. Lesson learned: optical change detection without corroborating radar displacement is prone to aerosol false alarms. Temporary desensitization proposals must be accompanied by blast-log correlation.

[INC-CASE-03] 2024-11-04 Zone 5 Un-alarmed Small Rockfall (Missed Event):
A 15 m3 block detached from an uninstrumented blind sector behind an equipment ramp during routine maintenance. No radar coverage in that sector. Discovered during weekly LiDAR drone difference mapping. Root cause: sensor coverage gap. Corrective action: deployed auxiliary PTZ camera and added event to golden evaluation benchmark.

[INC-CASE-04] 2025-02-18 Heavy Monsoonal Saturation (Tension Crack Dilation):
Continuous 48-hour rainfall event (110 mm total). Piezometers indicated 35% pore pressure surge. Forecaster flagged elevated risk (78% probability), but radar displacement was slow (0.8 mm/hr). Arbiter enforced precautionary exclusion based on sensor disagreement. Crest tension crack opened 14 hours later. Lesson: water-driven failures can precede kinematic radar acceleration.
