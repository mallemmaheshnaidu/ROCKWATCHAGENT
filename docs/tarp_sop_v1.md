# Trigger Action Response Plan (TARP) Standard Operating Procedures v1.0

[TARP-SOP-01] Level 1 (Green - Normal Monitoring):
Displacement velocity is less than 1.0 mm/hr and inverse velocity is stable or increasing. Normal operations continue. Standard radar scan cadence is 15 minutes. Routine visual inspections occur once per 12-hour shift.

[TARP-SOP-02] Level 2 (Yellow - Heightened Caution):
Displacement velocity is between 1.0 mm/hr and 5.0 mm/hr, or sustained rainfall exceeds 15 mm in a 3-hour window. Actions required: increase radar scan cadence to 5 minutes, notify pit supervisor and geotechnical engineer, restrict non-essential personnel from bench crest and toe, initiate continuous crack extensometer monitoring.

[TARP-SOP-03] Level 3 (Orange - Accelerated Deformation):
Displacement velocity is between 5.0 mm/hr and 15.0 mm/hr, or inverse-velocity plot exhibits a linear downward trend toward zero within 12 hours. Actions required: immediately stop all excavation and drilling in affected and adjacent zones, establish a physical safety berm and exclusion geofence at 1.5 times bench height, park heavy machinery outside the runout zone, conduct drone LiDAR mapping.

[TARP-SOP-04] Level 4 (Red - Imminent Failure):
Displacement velocity exceeds 15.0 mm/hr, acceleration is positive, or inverse velocity extrapolates to zero within 4 hours. Actions required: immediate audible siren evacuation of all personnel, haul trucks, and equipment within the 200m danger polygon; barricade access ramps; notify mine general manager; maintain remote radar monitoring; no re-entry permitted until 48 hours of geotechnical stability confirmation.

[TARP-SOP-05] Sensor Degradation Protocol:
If primary radar is offline or degraded, zone caution level must be automatically elevated by one tier (Green becomes Yellow; Yellow becomes Orange). Monitoring interval on secondary sensors (piezometers, extensometers, weather stations) must double in frequency.

[TARP-SOP-06] Inviolable Safety Floor:
Under no circumstances may an automated agent, operator, or software proposal override, desensitize, or lower the Level 4 Red evacuation trigger below 15.0 mm/hr. The TARP safety floor is absolute and legally binding under mine safety regulations.
